What are Kota Doria sarees and why are they considered the best lightweight sarees?

Kota Doria sarees (also called Kota Doriya or Kota cotton sarees) are handwoven using a specialized Khat square grid pattern. This creates an airy, featherlight drape that combines the strength of cotton with the sheen of silk, making them the most comfortable and elegant sarees for women.
